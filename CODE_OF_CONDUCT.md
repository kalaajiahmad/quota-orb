# Community standards

Be specific, constructive, and respectful. Technical disagreement and careful criticism are welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Use reproducible evidence and distinguish verified results from assumptions. Do not publish credentials, account data, personal paths, raw event payloads, or private logs. Obtain permission before sharing someone else's material.

Maintainers may request edits, remove inappropriate content, close off-topic discussions, or restrict abusive participants. Report inappropriate content through GitHub's reporting tools. For a security vulnerability, use the private reporting route in SECURITY.md instead of a public issue.
